## Month-by-Month Detail

### Month 1 — Launch
- **Goal:** 500 active users, 100 paid
- **Activities:**
  - Submit to Google Play Store
  - Launch GitHub Pages site
  - Post to Reddit (r/androidapps, r/familytech)
  - Family safety blogger outreach (5 influencers)
  - Product Hunt launch
- **Features live:** All 18 modules
- **Infrastructure cost:** $520

### Month 2 — Early Growth
- **Goal:** 950 MAU, 190 paid
- **Activities:**
  - First referral campaign ("Invite, get 1 month free")
  - Bug fixes from Month 1 user feedback
  - Apple App Store submission begins (iOS port planning)
  - First Play Store feature request to Google
- **New features:** Push notification improvements, widget support
- **Infrastructure cost:** $620

### Month 3 — Traction
- **Goal:** 2,000 MAU, 400 paid
- **Activities:**
  - App Store Optimization (ASO) — keyword tuning
  - Paid UA test: Google UAC campaign ($500 budget)
  - First care plan pilot with 2 daycare centers
  - User testimonials collected
- **New features:** Wear OS companion app (beta)
- **Infrastructure cost:** $820 (UAC + infra)

### Month 4 — Scaling
- **Goal:** 4,000 MAU, 800 paid
- **Activities:**
  - Google Play Featured App submission
  - Family + parenting podcast sponsorships (2)
  - Expand UAC spend to $1,500/month
  - Care plan → 10 active B2B clients
- **New features:** Health AI insights v2, location history timeline
- **Infrastructure cost:** $2,200 (UAC + infra)

### Month 5 — Acceleration
- **Goal:** 7,200 MAU, 1,440 paid
- **Activities:**
  - Press release to tech media (TechCrunch, 9to5Google, Android Authority)
  - iOS beta launch
  - Family safety score feature launch
  - Partner integration with 1 insurance company (pilot)
- **New features:** iOS companion app (beta), Smart Home integration
- **Infrastructure cost:** $3,200

### Month 6 — Established
- **Goal:** 11,500 MAU, 2,300 paid
- **Activities:**
  - iOS public launch
  - Series A funding pitch preparation
  - Enterprise care plan marketing materials
  - Hire first customer support contractor
- **New features:** Apple Watch support, family activity feed
- **Infrastructure cost:** $4,500

## Risk Factors

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Low Play Store visibility | Medium | ASO + UAC campaigns |
| High churn on free tier | Low | Referral program, strong onboarding |
| Google API quota limits | Low | Caching + quota management |
| Competition from Life360 | Medium | Differentiation via AI + walkie-talkie |
| Firebase cost spike | Low | Budget alerts + query optimization |

---

## Growth Assumptions
- Organic acquisition: 60% (word of mouth + ASO)
- Paid acquisition: 30% (UAC + influencers)
- Referral: 10%
- Monthly conversion rate (free→paid): 20%
- Retention at 30 days: 65%
- Retention at 90 days: 45%
