### Strategic Objectives
- 38,000 MAU, 7,600 paid subscribers
- $583K ARR run rate — Series A target: $3M at $15M valuation

### Key Activities
- Series A roadshow begins (5 VC meetings)
- Family4 featured in Google Play "Apps We Love" (targeting)
