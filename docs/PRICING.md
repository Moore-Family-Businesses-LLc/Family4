# Family4 — Pricing & Monetization Strategy

## Product Overview
**Family4** is a premium family safety and connectivity platform for Android (targeting Google Pixel 10 Pro XL). It bundles HDR camera, encrypted messaging, real-time location tracking, AI assistance, walkie-talkie, emergency SOS, health monitoring, and more into a single app.

---

## 1. Pricing Tiers

### 🆓 Free Tier — "Family Starter"
**Price:** $0/month

**Includes:**
- Dashboard with family status
- Notes (up to 50 notes)
- Calendar (personal events only)
- Basic camera (no HDR processing)
- Location sharing (1 member)
- 5 emergency SOS alerts/month
- 1GB Google Drive backup

**Limitations:**
- Ads in non-critical screens
- No encrypted chat
- No AI assistant
- No walkie-talkie
- No health tracking

---

### 💎 Family Plus — Core Plan
**Price:** $4.99/month | $47.99/year (20% savings)

**Includes everything in Free, plus:**
- Encrypted family chat (up to 6 members)
- HDR camera with Night Mode
- Full walkie-talkie mode (WebRTC P2P)
- Location tracking (up to 6 members, real-time)
- Unlimited notes + labels
- Full calendar with shared events + reminders
- Health tracking (steps, heart rate, sleep, water)
- Photo albums (shared family albums)
- Weather (live, location-aware)
- Tasks & to-do lists
- Google Drive backup (10GB)
- No ads
- FamilyBot AI (50 queries/month)

**Target user:** Families of 2–6 people wanting a complete connected experience.

---

### 👑 Family Premium — Full Power
**Price:** $9.99/month | $95.99/year (20% savings)

**Includes everything in Plus, plus:**
- Unlimited family members
- Unlimited FamilyBot AI queries (internet-capable)
- AI-generated notes, events, tasks from conversation
- Priority emergency response routing
- 50GB Google Drive backup
- Advanced health analytics + AI insights
- Historical location timeline (90 days)
- Priority customer support (24-hour response)
- Early access to new features
- Export all data as encrypted backup

**Target user:** Large families, caregivers, parents of young children, safety-focused households.

---

### 🏢 Family Business / Care Plan
**Price:** $24.99/month | $239.99/year

**Includes everything in Premium, plus:**
- Unlimited accounts under one billing
- Admin dashboard for account management
- Custom emergency contact routing
- API access for integrations (future)
- Dedicated support line
- SLA: 4-hour response time
- Custom branding options (family group name/icon)

**Target user:** Daycare centers, eldercare providers, group homes, large extended families.

## 6. Break-Even Analysis

- Fixed costs: ~$6,240/year (infrastructure)
- Break-even at: **~82 paid subscribers at blended $6.40/month**
- Expected to break even: **Month 1**
